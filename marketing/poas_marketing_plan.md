Polsia achieved viral top-of-funnel reach by producing short-form videos showcasing terminal windows scaffolding SaaS apps in 60 seconds and featuring animated C-Suite agent avatars (CEO, CTO, CMO). However, forensic inspection of Polsia's runtime infrastructure reveals four structural flaws that triggered brand collapse (1.7–3.0 Trustpilot ratings) and severe customer churn (>50%/month).

For an employed professional attempting to build a software side income stream in 15–30 minutes a day, these four structural flaws are lethal:
1. The **$1/Task Bug Tax** drains their personal card on automated error loops while they are in corporate meetings.
2. The **20%–50% Revenue Tax** destroys early-stage SaaS margin, requiring 40 hours/week of manual marketing just to break even.
3. The **Walled-Garden Hostage Model** denies them true code ownership on their personal GitHub, keeping their domain asset captive.
4. **"False Done" Hallucinations** force them to spend their scarce evening hours troubleshooting Next.js blank screens and unwired Stripe webhooks.

#### Flaw 1: The $1/Task "Bug Tax" & Adversarial Monetization
Polsia operates on an asynchronous Celery and Redis queue architecture. Users pay a $49/mo base subscription and purchase additional task credits at ~$1.00 each. A "task" is defined as any Celery worker execution or LLM agent tool call.
* When an agent generates invalid TypeScript (e.g. missing `'use server'` in Next.js App Router), the runtime errors.
* Polsia's orchestration engine triggers an automatic error-repair prompt.
* **Polsia bills the user's credit ledger for every remediation attempt caused by its own agent's mistakes.**
* In documented real-world audits, an agent entering an 18-step circular retry loop over hallucinated npm packages burned **$23.00 in user credits** before crashing permanently with an unhandled database reset.
* *Economic Perversion:* The platform extracts higher gross revenue the more buggy and incompetent its AI models are.

#### Flaw 2: The 20%–50% Perpetual Revenue Tax & 20% Ad Surcharge
Polsia extracts between 20% and 50% of gross revenue processed through customer applications and adds a 20% markup on all Meta and Google ad spend managed by its CMO agent.
* *Mathematical Proof of Venture Destruction:*
  - Consider a $50/mo SaaS with $5/mo COGS (10%) and a $100 customer acquisition cost (CAC).
  - On an independent Stripe setup, founder net monthly margin is $43.25 (86.5%), yielding a 2.31-month payback and an LTV:CAC of **4.33x**.
  - Under Polsia’s 50% revenue tax and 20% ad markup ($120 effective CAC), founder net monthly cash flow drops to **$18.25** (36.5% margin), extending payback to **6.58 months**.
  - Incorporating platform instability churn (15% monthly customer drop-off), effective customer lifetime is 6.67 months. Total net cash flow per customer is $121.73, yielding a net profit of **$1.73 per customer** and an LTV:CAC ratio of **1.01x**.
  - *Conclusion:* Polsia consumes **98.6% of early-stage venture gross profit**, making sustainable profitability mathematically impossible.

#### Flaw 3: Proprietary Walled-Garden Hostage Model & Zero Code Ownership
Polsia stores customer code inside closed multi-tenant Docker container volumes routed through a shared Traefik reverse proxy.
* No continuous dual-push to user-owned GitHub or GitLab repositories.
* No exportable database migration scripts, raw PostgreSQL dumps, or Terraform manifests.
* If a customer cancels their $49/mo plan or disputes an unauthorized credit charge:
  1. Traefik routes are instantly revoked (domains return `502 Bad Gateway`).
  2. Container instances are terminated and deleted from disk.
  3. Database volumes are destroyed, permanently wiping customer user tables and billing histories.
  4. The founder walks away with $0 in enterprise value.

#### Flaw 4: "False Done" Hallucinations
Polsia’s verification layer evaluates task completion using process exit codes and shallow HTTP 200 headers (`curl -I http://localhost:3000`):
* **Next.js Blank DOM Mirage:** Next.js App Router renders an empty root container (`<div id="__next"></div>`) with an HTTP `200 OK` header before hydration. When client JavaScript crashes on missing environment variables or type mismatches, the user sees a completely blank white screen. Polsia logs the task as "COMPLETED."
* **Unwired Stripe Webhooks:** Agents generate visual Stripe Elements forms but leave unconfigured webhook secrets (`STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET="whsec_placeholder"`). Customers swipe credit cards, Stripe collects payments, but the webhook fails HMAC signature validation (HTTP 400). The database never provisions user accounts, sparking immediate dispute disputes and chargebacks.
* **Cryptographic SSL Handshake Failures:** Automated domain routing fails to attach full intermediate CA certificate chains or configure Subject Alternative Names (SANs) under RFC 6125, triggering aggressive browser security blocks (`NET::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID`).
